## Features

- **Enrichment map**: the enriched terms as a flow out of their source's levels (KEGG category › group, Reactome pathway levels, COG areas, GO aspect, with GO terms nested by GO's own hierarchy) into gene modules.
- **Set comparison**: compare sets of genes or enriched terms as an UpSet plot, or a Venn of two or three sets.
- **Several reference proteins**: normalise to a set of reference proteins, combined by median or geometric mean, with a choice for samples missing one (stop, drop the sample, or use the ones measured). The picker lists the proteins before anything has run.
- **Model view for curve fits**: a **Model** switch above the plots below a Curve fit shows either the **detector** (what was tested) or the **descriptor** (the 4PL, line or joint description).
- **Force pEC50**: potency, curve metrics and curve QC can place flat curves at pEC50 0 instead of leaving them out.
- **Enrichment FDR cut**: enrichment plots can draw only terms below an FDR, still capped at the top N.

## Fixes

- **Exported legends are complete**: a download or copy of a plot whose legend scrolls on screen now shows the whole legend, below the plot, with the plot itself exactly as shown.
- **Smoother after a settings change**: undo copies of large results are saved in the background in small pieces, so the app no longer freezes for a second after each change.
- **Faster large plots**: QC violins and boxes, heatmap clustering, contrasts and re-applying a threshold no longer recompute on the UI thread.
- **Projects with removed plots** open, leaving out the tiles this version can't draw.

## Behaviour changes

- **Plot control bar per plot type**: point plots keep zoom, pan, box / lasso select and reset; curve plots zoom, pan and reset; heatmaps, bars, networks and other category plots have no control bar and no drag-zoom. 3-D plots rotate by turntable only.
- **Configure curve fit**: intent and axis are one **curve** choice — the joint dose × time surface, dose curves at each time, or time curves at each dose. The two axes' models sit side by side.
