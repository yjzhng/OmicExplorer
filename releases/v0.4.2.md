## Features

- **Consistent plot settings**: every plot's settings window follows one order: what the plot shows, the statistic from its upstream step, labels, points and lines, colours, then Legend and Axes. Each plot has one Colour section.
- **Legend section on every plot**: volcano, MA, scatter, potency and curve-fit QC now switch their legend in a Legend section like the other plots. Each group's own legend entry stays in Points.
- **Cap labels on MA plots**: label only the top genes by fold change, as on volcano and scatter plots.
- **Axes on Curve metrics**: the bar charts take the same axis settings as the other plots.
- **Sample order on Correlation**: with clustering off, samples sort by condition (natural) or follow the data's own order.
- **One text scale for plots**: plot and axis titles 13 px, ticks and legends 11 px, gene labels and annotations 10 px, and 9 px only for dense heatmap and matrix labels.
- **Consistent controls and motion**: sections, fields, number boxes, tickboxes and switches look and behave the same in plot settings, dialogs and Settings. Animations share one set of timings, and the system's Reduce motion setting now stills all of them.
- **Error reporting**: Help → Copy Diagnostics copies recent errors with the app and system details, and Help → Show Log File opens the app's log. A plot or settings panel that fails shows an error in its place instead of blanking the window.

## Behaviour changes

- Correlation with clustering off now sorts samples by condition by default. Choose "original" for the data's own order.
- Plot ticks and legends are slightly smaller (11 px, was 12), and gene labels slightly larger (10 px, was 9).
