## Features

- **Curve fit is its own step**, beside Compare. Old curve-fit Compares open as Curve fit tiles.
  - **Models**: **4PL** (CurveCurator) or **Hybrid**, which detects responses with a spline and describes them with a 4PL.
  - **Axis**: dose, time, or a **dose × time** surface, drawn by the new **Dose & Time response** plot in 2D or 3D.
  - **Curve-fit QC**: residual and curve-metric checks.
- **New Compare, Contrast and Curve fit windows**: one row per condition. Each condition is compared, matched, shared or pooled, and values can be left out with a click.
- **Contrasts**: compare SD, CV, significance or pEC50 as well as the main value. Drag cutoffs on the scatter.
- **Subset step**: keeps samples whose conditions take chosen values.
- **Normalisation** in Clean data: median, or median and spread.
- **Palettes**: a default per role in Settings → Customisation, custom palettes, and a palette per plot.
- **Linked highlighting**: samples, heatmap rows and columns, enrichment terms and STRING clusters highlight across plots.
- **Fold-change heatmap clustering**: linkage, leaf order, dendrograms and gene modules.
- **Project templates**: save a project's structure without data, and start new projects from it.
- **Undo restores results** instead of needing a re-run.
- **Settings → Database**: manage the shared UniProt and STRING cache.
- **Faster**: parallel workers for curve fits and enrichment, and WebGL for dense plots.

## Fixes

- **Curve fits with several drugs**: each drug now uses its own dose 0.
- **Dose-response from a Compare on dose**: no longer split into one tab per dose.
- **Correlated contrast**: the band and the calls now agree.
- **Volcano**: the axis and boundary curves no longer cut off the cutoff guides.

## Behaviour changes

- **Samplesheets need a `sample` column**: `well`, `position` and `plate` are no longer read. Every condition in use must be filled, and dose and time must be numbers.
- **Wide or long is detected from the columns**, so no `_wide` / `_long` suffix is needed. `locus` replaces `locus_tag`, and CEG/NEG essentiality is removed.
- **Existing results may change after a re-run**:
  - **Correlated contrasts** call against the fitted line instead of y = x.
  - **Fold-change heatmaps** reorder (Ward linkage).
  - **The cluster plot** defaults to "complete" missing values.
- **Gene groups belong to the project**, shared by all workflows.
