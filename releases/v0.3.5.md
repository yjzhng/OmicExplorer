## Features

- **Curve fit analysis (CurveCurator)**: Compare has a third analysis, **Curve fit**, next to t-test and 2-way ANOVA. It fits one curve per gene across all doses, instead of one test per dose.
  - **Model**: a 4-parameter log-logistic curve over log₁₀ dose, fitted to each sample's ratio to the control mean, and tested against a flat line with CurveCurator's recalibrated F-test. A gene keeps the flat model (`M0`) when the F-test doesn't reject it.
  - **Curve axis**: any numeric condition (dose by default), with at least 4 levels above the control. Doses at or below 0 are left out, with a note in the preview.
  - **Results**: the row's fold change is the curve's, labelled "curve log₂FC". The table and exports add EC50 (in the dose unit, e.g. `EC50_uM`), pEC50, slope, front and back plateaus, R², RMSE, points, F and model.
  - **CurveCurator threshold**: calls by relevance score, at an α cutoff or a target FDR from CurveCurator's target–decoy method. The estimated FDR of the current calls is shown, and re-thresholding needs no re-run. Switching to Curve fit starts from CurveCurator's defaults (α 0.05, |curve log₂FC| ≥ 0.45).
  - **New plots**: **Potency** (pEC50 against curve log₂FC, significant curves by default) and **Residual plots** (a violin of every protein's residual at each dose, for curve-fit QC).
  - **Dose-response**: fitted curves are drawn on a continuous log-dose axis, and the focused view shows a gene's curve, measured points, 95% confidence band and EC50.
- **SAM with permutation FDR** (Tusher 2001, as Perseus runs it): a new significance method for t-test Compares. d = Δ / (s + s0), with s0 = 0.1 and 250 seeded label shuffles by default, and a permutation q-value. The volcano draws SAM's hyperbolic cutoff. SAM always tests on log₂ values.
- **Significance method**: the Statistic section starts with a method: **P-value** (FDR control, BH or Bonferroni, linear or hyperbolic boundary), **SAM** (t-test) or **CurveCurator** (curve fit).
- **Analyses checked against the data**: an analysis the data can't support is greyed out, with the reason on hover. A t-test needs replicates on both sides, a 2-way ANOVA two conditions with at least 2 values each, a curve fit at least 4 dose or time levels. The analysis can now also be changed from the Compare's settings panel.
- **Condition units**: dose (M, mM, µM, nM, pM) and time (s, min, h, d, wk) units, or a.u., are set at import. Interactive import asks for every dose and time condition; a samplesheet loaded by hand sets them under "Condition units" on the Load tile. Units show in axis titles such as `dose (nM)` and in EC50 labels. Changing a unit never puts a result out of date.
- **Effect colours on Compare**: up, down and none each get a colour, and "none" can be renamed. Every plot fed by the Compare uses them unless the plot sets its own; Automatic goes back to following the Compare.
- **Plots have a Run**: a plot tile shows "Not run yet" with a **Run** button until it has run, a spinner while running, and its error with **Retry** if it fails.
  - **Enrichment and STRING**: one run covers every method, term source, confidence level, gene cap and interactor mode, so changing these only redraws. Only a new STRING species or a custom gene cap needs a re-run.
  - **Plot groups**: each plot has its own status dot and a Run chip on hover.
- **Run All**: runs branch by branch, so a finished branch can be viewed while the rest run. "Running…" shows beside the Stop button.
- **Dose/time-response**:
  - **Views**: global (every gene) or focused (one gene at a time with its points, SE error bars and, for a curve fit, the fitted curve).
  - **Style**: line, point and error look; error as a band or whiskers; value axis global or per gene.
  - **Overview**: the selection colours its curves in place of the top genes.
  - **Response comparison (Contrast)**: replicate points, band or whisker error, and a pager over every gene until some are selected.
- **Gene bar plot**: SD, SEM or 95% CI error bars; optional replicate dots; colour by condition (a categorical condition sets the colour, dose or time the shade); a per-gene or global axis that fits the data instead of starting at 0; values on Clean data's scale.
- **QC plots**: one fill colour or colour by condition. Protein-count bars stack the imputed proteins, lighter, on top of the measured ones. The axis can fit the bars' range.
- **Cluster plot**: settings regrouped into Plot, Data, Colour and Display. Marker and centroid size, opacity and outline, arrow trail width, opacity and head size, and markers under trails can be set. In the complex legend you choose which conditions drive colour, shade and arrows.
- **Heatmap**: a "show conditions" toggle for the condition bar. Long gene labels are truncated to fit, with the full name on hover.
- **Volcano**: SAM's |d| cutoff and CurveCurator's relevance boundary are drawn, fixed by the Compare's settings. When the selection is several gene groups, volcano, MA and scatter draw each as its own trace with its own legend entry.
- **Smarter labels**: volcano labels rank by distance from the origin, with fold change and significance each scaled to its axis. MA labels rank by |log₂FC|, scatter labels by distance from y = x or from the centre.
- **Enrichment plot**:
  - **Category chips**: click legend chips to show only those groups' terms.
  - **Dot style**: a dot-size key, with the up and down colourbars stacked under it.
  - **Axis**: the title names the measure and the comparison, with the up and down counts at the ends.
  - **Highlighting**: a term's label goes bold while it, or one of its GSEA genes, is hovered or pinned.
- **STRING**: confidence levels with no interactions among the query genes are greyed out. With no level set, the tile picks the highest level that still has a network.
- **Export data (Enrichment, STRING)**: a new tile button saves the data for every facet as CSV or XLSX. STRING writes `<name>_nodes` and `<name>_edges`.
- **Canvas groups (frames)**: Group draws a labelled, shaded frame around the selected tiles that moves them together. Frames can nest. Double-click a label to rename it, and drag-select picks up a whole group. Ungroup removes the frame and keeps the tiles.
- **Groups in Form mode**: a group is a shaded block with a fold arrow, a renamable header and a drag handle. ⌘/Ctrl-click and Shift-click select several steps to group them.
- **Tidy**: lays the selected tiles out left to right along their connections, with fewer crossings. It can be undone.
- **Canvas cues**:
  - **Incomplete tiles**: dashed outline.
  - **Connections**: green when both ends are complete, red into an errored step.
  - **"+" handle**: appears on the output after a hover, and dragging from it adds the next step.
  - **Plot groups**: can be folded.
- **Interactive import**:
  - **Conditions step**: Conditions and Samplesheet are merged into one step, with a switch per condition and a "custom" row. Curved arrows show which sample-name tokens each condition reads.
  - **Separator**: picked from a list or typed.
  - **Renaming**: a rename bar that says how many samples will change.
  - **Hand edits**: outlined.
  - **Data files**: the list shows each file's folder.
  - **Help**: (i) tips explain each step.
- **Gene selector**:
  - **Search**: shows a flat list of matching genes above the tabs and narrows the tree.
  - **Paging**: long categories page 100 pathways at a time.
  - **Gene groups**: "geneset" is now "gene group". While you edit a group, picks in plots and tables add to it until **Confirm**. Clicking several groups combines them.
  - **Tile gene pickers**: list and search protein descriptions.
- **Data tables**: matrix headers show one line per condition with its name, and rows fade at the scroll edges.
- **Rename a Results tab**: click the open tab to rename its step.
- **Project data folder**: `<project>_data/<workflow>/input` and `output/<step>`, one output folder per step.
  - **Renames**: renaming a project, workflow or step renames its folder on the next save.
  - **Unsaved projects**: work in a scratch folder that moves into place on the first save.
  - **Save as**: copies the data folder.
  - **Downloads and exports**: open in the step's `output/` folder.
- **Project folder viewer**: a nav button that browses the data folder and opens a file or shows it in Finder / File Explorer.
- **Large projects**: project files are smaller (compact, gzipped, result tables stored as columns) and can exceed JavaScript's string limit. Workflows not on screen are kept on disk until opened, which lowers memory use.
- **Home page and About**: a namecard with the logo, version, Website and GitHub links, and "Cite this software" with a copy button.
- **Settings → Appearance**: colour scheme, a new accent colour (Graphite, Blue, Orange or Custom) and the Layout choice. Flow/Form can also be switched from the nav bar.
- **User Guide rewritten**: sections with schematic illustrations that follow light and dark mode.
- **"Working…" notice**: shown while the app is busy, so a long pause doesn't look like a hang.
- **Non-fatal notes**: a tile can warn without failing, e.g. a missing optional DB.
- **README**: reference tables for the data file (wide or long), samplesheet and DB columns.

## Fixes

- **App froze after a condition switch or re-run**: every tile rebuilt at once. Tiles now update one per frame, on-screen tiles first, with the old plot dimmed under a spinner until its turn.
- **Gene selector lag**: it regrouped every gene each time a plot stored a result, and a search could freeze the app.
- **Wiring a second Compare** to Clean data emptied the first Compare and its plots.
- **Save after a run**: Save stayed disabled after a run, and closing didn't offer to save.
- **Truncated project files**: a save now goes to a temporary file first and replaces the project only when complete.
- **Deleted data folder**: writes and exports no longer quietly re-create a data folder deleted during the session; it is flagged as missing.
- **macOS quit**: closing the window now quits the app instead of leaving it in the Dock.
- **QC protein count**: counts measured values only. With imputation on, every sample used to show the same count.
- **Laggy plot settings window**: editing a Compare's statistic from a plot's settings re-called genes on every keystroke. Those edits now wait for **Apply**.
- **Plot crashes with ~100k points**: DR, volcano, heatmap and loadings could overflow the stack.
- **Points missing after zoom**, and **stuck hover** after a zoom box, reset or redraw.
- **Enrichment**: selecting a gene elsewhere dimmed the whole plot, and a hovered edge dot nudged the axis.
- **Bar highlighting**: bars dim bar by bar instead of drawing stray overlay dots.
- **Log-axis settings**: range and tick interval are taken in data units, e.g. 1–1000.
- **Condition names**: Compare, 2-way ANOVA and contrast previews show display names, not internal keys.
- **Blurry text**: in the comparison dialog's preview tables and the tile picker.
- **Panel settings dialog**: wider, so cutoff rows no longer scroll sideways, and its buttons stay in view.
- **Delete button**: was nearly invisible in light mode.
- **Steps added in Form mode**: they stacked in one column on the canvas.
- **Memory**: undo history no longer copies every node at each step, and deleting a gene group no longer re-runs every Compare and Contrast.
- **Spinners**: they keep turning while the app is busy.
- **Disabled options**: they now explain why on hover.

## Behaviour changes

- **Projects saved by 0.3.5 can't be opened by older versions.** Older projects still open in 0.3.5. From now on a project records the app version that saved it. Opening a project saved by a newer version shows a warning that you may experience issues, naming the version to update to, and then opens it.
- **Compare tests on Clean data's scale**: on log values when Clean data's log-transform is log₂ or log₁₀, on the values as they are otherwise. The separate "log₂-transform before testing" switch is gone, and changing the log-transform now re-runs downstream Compares. SAM always uses log₂; curve fits always fit ratios of linear values.
- **No default log-transform**: Clean data no longer picks log₁₀ for raw intensities. **Set it on purpose**: raw intensities left at none are tested and plotted linear. For projects that relied on the old default, set Clean data's log-transform to log₁₀ and re-run.
- **Abundance plots follow Clean data's log-transform**: heatmap, bars, MA, abundance scatter, contrasts, QC, sample correlation, and PCA/clustering on "auto". The heatmap's own log switch is gone. Axes read "log abundance", "log₂ abundance" or "abundance".
- **Bubble and dose/time-response top genes**: only genes significant at one or more levels count. N is split between up and down in proportion.
- **Plots must be run**: a plot is complete only once it has run, and Run All includes plots.
- **Every plot is a plot group**: a lone plot is a group of one, and older projects open that way. Add or remove plots from the tile's add-plot menu.
- **Group / Ungroup now frame tiles** on the canvas, instead of merging plots into a plot group.
- **Run All carries on past a failure**: a failed step only skips the steps below it.
- **Panel settings Apply**: a change that needs a re-run waits for **Apply**. Close drops it.
- **No background `temp/` files**: the 0.3.4 background saves of Enrichment and STRING results are replaced by **Export data**.
- **Export folders**: one per processing step, named after it.
- **New tiles stay closed** on the canvas, and leaving Form mode tidies the canvas (undoable).
- **Plots that need per-dose rows or group means** (bubble, TDR, MA, scatter) don't accept a curve fit and say why.
- **Wording**: "Select genes" is now "Highlight genes". Plot gene labels are cut at 10 characters (was 20).
- **Dose-response** no longer has the All/Selected toggle.
