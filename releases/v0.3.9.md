## Features

- **Legend switch** on every plot without points (heatmaps, PCA, QC, correlation, enrichment, bubble, gene bars, response plots …): turn the legend and colour bars off.
- **Finer Contrast tails**: the tail % goes down to 0.001%, in the field, by dragging the band, and with the arrow keys.
- **Faster on large results**:
  - Tables, facets, heatmaps, QC, sample correlation, gene bars and the Clean data matrix read the stored results directly, using less memory.
  - Big tables draw only the rows on screen, so they scroll smoothly.
  - Volcano, MA and Contrast scatter draw more than 100,000 non-significant points as a shaded density. Labelled, pinned and hovered genes stay as dots.
  - Dashboard tiles draw one at a time, and the fold-change heatmap re-clusters in the background with the previous one kept on screen.

## Fixes

- **Clean data matrix**: two samples whose conditions read the same when joined (dose 1 + time 24, dose 12 + time 4) stay two columns.
- **Small tiles and facets** no longer fail with "Something went wrong with axis scaling", and a plot's spinner no longer stays up after a failed draw.
- **Heatmap**: the gene side no longer jumps as it opens.
- **Enrichment**: picking a group no longer moves the axis.
- **Volcano**: cutoff guides reach the plot edges when you zoom out or pan.
- **Settings sections** no longer fold when you click a button in their header.

## Behaviour changes

- **Curve fits change after a re-run**: each curve is now described by a 4PL fitted on log₂ ratios, so pEC50, slope, plateaus, curve log₂FC and the confidence band change, and calls near the fold-change cutoff can flip. CurveCurator's test (F, p, R²) still uses the fit on ratios. Saved results show as before until re-run.
- **Contrast cutoffs** set by dragging are kept to 0.001%, so labels can show more decimals.
- **STRING confidence** is chosen by its score cutoff (≥ 0.15, 0.40, 0.70, 0.90) instead of low / medium / high / highest.
- **Volcano** has no autoscale button: double-click resets to the plot's own range.
- **Plot look**: axis lines, gridlines and cutoff guides are thinner (0.5 px), and 2-D plots are framed on all four sides unless turned off. The fitted-line Contrast scatter no longer draws y = x, and Enrichment no longer labels the down / up counts under its axis.
