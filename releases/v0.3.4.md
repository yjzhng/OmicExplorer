## Features

- **Protein groups**: a `;`-joined protein group (e.g. from MaxQuant, DIA-NN or Spectronaut) is now one feature throughout, as it was measured.
  - **Statistics**: the group counts once in Compare, volcano and every plot. Previously it was split into one copy per member.
  - **Labels**: a group is labelled by all its members' genes, e.g. `HDAC1/HDAC2`.
  - **Annotations**: fetched for every member protein. A group carries the union of its members' terms, so it appears under every pathway, function and essentiality class any member has.
  - **Isoforms**: isoform IDs (`P12345-2`) fold into their UniProt entry for fetching and matching. Stripping uses UniProt's accession format, so gene symbols like `NKX2-1` are left alone.
  - **Import DB**: one row per protein, keyed by its accession.
  - **STRING**: each member is its own node, and a group's nodes highlight together.
- **Missing values**: 0, blank, `NA` and `NaN` intensities all count as not measured. A samplesheet dose of 0 stays a real value.
- **Annotation sources, reorganised**:
  - **KEGG**: each pathway carries its pathway category and pathway group (`KEGG`, `KEGG_cat`, `KEGG_grp`).
  - **Reactome**: three levels. The tested term is the level-3 pathway (lowest-level pathways are rolled up to it, since they're too small to enrich on), with its level-2 group and top-level category (`Reactome`, `Reactome_grp`, `Reactome_cat`).
  - **COG and KOG**: separate fetch options and separate columns (`COG` / `COG_cat` / `COG_area`, `KOG` / `KOG_cat` / `KOG_area`). Prokaryotic proteins land in COG, eukaryotic ones in KOG.
  - **Renamed columns**: `MSigDB`, `Reactome`, `DEG` and `CEG_NEG`. Older column names, including a prokaryotic DB's `KG_PW`, are still read.
  - **Labels**: the fetch options and selectors use DEG, CEG-NEG (human), KEGG, Reactome (human), GO, COG, KOG (human) and MSigDB.
- **Gene selector**:
  - **Groupings**: KEGG shows pathway category → pathway group → pathway, Reactome shows top level → level 2 → level-3 term, and GO terms sit under their aspect (biological process / molecular function / cellular component).
  - **Measured genes only**: it lists exactly the features that came through Clean data.
- **Enrichment**:
  - **KOG** is its own term source.
  - **GO terms are grouped by aspect.**
  - **Group squares in every style**: the dot and bar styles now show each term's group as a coloured square with a legend, as the ridge style did. The legend is left-aligned and wraps to the tile's width.
- **Results saved to temp**: Enrichment and STRING tiles write their full results to the project's `temp/` folder in the background. A tile's status shows its progress.
  - **Enrichment**: every facet and every tested term.
  - **STRING**: node and edge tables with modules.
  - **Kept current**: files only update when the upstream results or relevant settings change, and stay current across restarts.
- **Interactive import**:
  - **Column labelling**: Shift-click labels every column between the last one clicked and this one. A drag can run past the table's edge, scrolling it.
  - **Metadata page**: arrows step a wide table one column at a time. The vertical scrollbar starts below the header. The preview shows the DB as it will be written, one row per protein. The fetch status sits beside the Fetch buttons, in warning style while running and success or warning style when done.
- **Clean data — Log-transform**: each histogram reports **normal** or **non-normal** from its shape. Switching none / log10 / log2 is now instant.
- **Plots**:
  - **Abundance axes autoscale**: the MA plot's abundance axis and abundance-vs-abundance scatters fit the data instead of starting at 0.
  - **Long labels truncated**: gene labels in bubble and dose/time-response plots are cut at 20 characters with "…", and the full name shows on hover.
  - **Dose/time-response**: shows the top 10 genes by default.
- **Load data**: the Interactive / Manual choice uses the app's standard toggle switch.

## Fixes

- **KEGG pathway matching**: KEGG gene IDs from UniProt are now split on `;` before matching KEGG's pathway links. A trailing `;`, or a protein with several KEGG gene IDs, could stop them matching and leave proteins without pathways. The "Resolving KEGG pathways" step now shows its progress, runs several requests at once, and skips a failed batch instead of failing the fetch.
- **Re-fetch left stale values**: a re-fetch could leave old annotation values in place; it now replaces them.
- **GSEA extreme NES**: large gene sets could report NES of about 10⁸ with p = 1. This happened when none of the random permutations shared the observed score's sign. NES and p are now computed against all permutations in that case.
- **Enrichment hover**: hovering an enrichment dot drew a far larger highlight than the dot itself.
- **Slow log-transform switch**: changing Clean data's log-transform made every downstream plot rebuild, taking 5–10 s on large data.
- **COG areas mispaired**: COG categories could be paired with the wrong functional area when annotations were combined.

## Behaviour changes

- **Re-fetch annotations in existing projects** to get the new columns: the COG/KOG split, KEGG pathway category and group, and Reactome levels. Then re-run Clean data and Compare.
- **Reactome terms are level-3 pathways**, so Reactome enrichment results change.
- **Zero intensities count as missing**, which can change clean-up coverage, imputation and statistics for data that recorded missing values as 0.
- **Feature IDs**: a protein group keeps its original ID. A row naming a single protein, isoforms of one entry included, takes that protein's accession.
