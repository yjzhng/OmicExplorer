## Features

- **Interactive tutorial**: a new user's first project offers a quick tour (a light-bulb card at the bottom right: **Show me** or **Not now**). The tour runs on its own synthetic example data and shows each step on screen: placing and linking steps, settings, renaming, running, making a volcano, and exploring the results. Your project is put aside meanwhile and comes back as it was. Replay it any time from the User Guide: **Revisit interactive tutorial**.
- **First-step guide**: on an empty workflow, the new step's type picker says what to choose.
- **Manual data input** shows where the three files go: the data folder, its `input/` folder and the files in it.
- **Back from Results** highlights the tile of the tab you were on, and jumping to a tab from a tile highlights its tab.
- **Results tiles pack upwards** as you move or resize them. Dragging a tile along its row swaps it with its neighbour, and the snap dots line up with the tiles.

## Fixes

- **A new project no longer shows as saved** (green dot) before it has a file. Save works before the first edit.
- **Plots in small tiles no longer fail** with "Something went wrong with axis scaling": colour bars shrink, lose their title or hide to fit. A failed tile tries again when it's resized or its data changes.
- **A tile's "+"** no longer keeps animating after the tile moves out from under the cursor.

## Behaviour changes

- **Folder menu**: the "Select folder where project data is saved to" row is gone; **＋ Add folder…** does the same.
- **Load data file menus** read **Select data…** / **Select file…** when empty, instead of "—". Metadata, being optional, has **No metadata** to clear it.
